Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you create — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and general usage data, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate your account, display your expense history and summaries, and improve the features of the Service. Your data is used solely to provide you with the functionality of Spendly and is not used for advertising or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored securely in our database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. While we work to keep your data safe, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the Service, and these providers only process data as necessary to keep Spendly running.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
